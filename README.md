# Kinetix

Kinetix is a self-hosted AI personal trainer. It plans your training and nutrition from your
weigh-ins and meal photos, reviews your set videos, talks with you in English through your earphones,
and ships as an iPhone app. Your data stays in a folder you own.

## Based on openGym

Kinetix is a fork of [openGym](https://github.com/DuarteSantos8/openGym), a self-hosted gym tracker.
The upstream project, its history and its licence (AGPL-3.0-or-later) are kept. See [LICENSE](LICENSE)
and [NOTICE.md](NOTICE.md). Exercise media are licensed separately (NOTICE.md).
